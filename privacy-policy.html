Flint · 燧石行动

隐私政策

生效日期：2026 年 10 月 14 日

简体中文

燧石行动当前版本不要求创建账号，不提供在线匹配，不包含广告或应用内分析服务。依据当前应用源码和依赖审查，游戏不通过应用收集个人信息，也不将玩家资料传送给开发者或其他第三方。此政策应在每次发布前根据最终归档应用及其嵌入组件复核，并与 App Store 隐私披露保持一致。

游戏设置（例如音量、语言与触觉反馈）保存在本机应用偏好中；等级、经验、模式统计与最近战绩保存在本机应用沙盒内。当前版本不将这些数据同步到服务器。删除应用通常会移除本机应用数据；如果从包含该应用数据的 Apple 设备备份恢复，Apple 可能会恢复备份中保留的数据。本游戏没有账号或服务器端玩家资料，因此无需向开发者申请删除服务器数据。

游戏中的素材署名链接仅在玩家主动点击时打开 Sketchfab 等第三方页面。打开后，第三方网站可能依据其自己的隐私政策处理浏览器访问数据；燧石行动不控制这些网站。

隐私或游戏支持：duscott80@gmail.com。

English

The current version of Flint does not require an account, offer online matchmaking, or include advertising or in-app analytics. Based on a review of the current app source and dependencies, the game does not collect personal information through the app or transmit player profile data to the developer or other parties. This policy must be rechecked against the final archived app and its embedded components before each release, and must match the App Store privacy disclosure.

Settings such as audio, language, and haptics are stored in local app preferences. Level, experience, mode statistics, and recent match history are stored in the app's local container. The current version does not sync this data to a server. Deleting the app normally removes its local app data; Apple may restore data retained in a device backup if you restore from that backup. Flint has no account or server-side player profile, so there is no server-held player data to request deletion of from the developer.

In-game attribution links open third-party pages such as Sketchfab only when a player chooses to tap them. Those websites may process browser visit data under their own privacy policies; Flint does not control those websites.

For privacy or game support, contact duscott80@gmail.com.

政策更新 · Changes

若未来版本增加联网、账号、分析或其他数据处理方式，政策与 App Store 隐私披露须在发布该版本前同步更新。 If a future version adds networking, accounts, analytics, or other data practices, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.
